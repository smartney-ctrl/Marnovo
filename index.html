Our Story

Crafted with Intention

Marnavo was founded on a simple belief: quality tailoring shouldn't be limited by geography. From our first stitch, we set out to build a shirt house that could serve one boutique or one thousand — without ever compromising on craftsmanship.

Every Marnavo piece is designed in our signature palette of deep navy, warm sand, and muted gold — a nod to timeless tailoring with a distinctly modern edge.

Today, Marnavo supplies retail customers and wholesale partners across multiple states, with an eye on expanding worldwide.
